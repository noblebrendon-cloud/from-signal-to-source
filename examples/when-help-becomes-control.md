# Worked Example — When Help Becomes Control

This example records the shape of a Signal-to-Source run that began with a live discussion about union organizing and the mediator's role.

## Signal

A union organizer described organizing as creating the material conditions that allow workers to organize themselves. The transferable pattern was that a mediator can increase another actor's capacity without becoming the authoritative actor.

Working transition:

`Worker₀ → resources / knowledge / coordination → Worker₁`

## Initial thesis

A mediator becomes paternalistic when helping another person exercise agency turns into exercising agency on that person's behalf.

## Research challenge

Research across paternalism theory, capability approaches, self-determination, medicine, disability rights, participation, labor organizing, and subsidiarity broadly supported the pattern but challenged a simplistic equation between capability gain and legitimate mediation.

## Refinement

The framework separated three variables that had initially been collapsed:

`Capability ≠ Authority ≠ Goal Ownership`

A stronger invariant emerged:

> The principal should remain the legitimate author of the principal's ends unless a bounded and independently justified transfer of authority exists.

The refined diagnostic distinguished expertise from authority:

`Knowledge(Mediator) > Knowledge(Principal)`

**does not imply**

`Authority(Mediator) > Authority(Principal)`

## Publication

The resulting article was published as:

*When Help Becomes Control: The Point Where Mediation Turns Paternalistic*

The article also introduced three working diagnostics:

- Agency Substitution Test
- Goal Ownership Test
- Mediator Dependency Test

These labels were presented as proposed analytical formulations, not standardized academic terminology.

## Distribution

Platform-native rollout copy was then created for Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and Threads, with the constraint that no social post could make a stronger claim than the canonical article.

## Why this example matters

The method is recursive: a live conversation became an article, and the successful article-development process was then abstracted into the versioned workflow specification contained in this repository.
